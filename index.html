PROJECT STATUS

Veritas Hub is a work in progress

Veritas Hub is an independent student project, and development is ongoing. Some features are still being tested. These known limitations are priorities for future improvements, not the end of the project.

Known issues under development
  • Some areas save data only in this browser, so it may not appear on another device.
  • The keke planner saves a trip note; it does not dispatch a driver or confirm a real fare.
  • Shared posts and feedback depend on Supabase connectivity and database permissions.
  • Marketplace contact opens WhatsApp; the site does not process payments or verify sellers and listings.
  • Campus place labels and route information are a pilot and need student verification before being treated as official directions.
  • Community ratings and issue reports are submitted by users and are not independently verified.

What happens next? These features will be improved as testing, feedback and resources allow. Students can help by trying available features and reporting problems. Please verify important campus directions and transport details independently while the relevant features are being tested.

Prototype limitations identified in a code review

These are known limitations from reviewing the project code, not a claim that every one was reproduced in a live browser test.

  • Some areas still save data only in the current browser, so they will not automatically appear on another device.
  • The keke planner saves a trip note; it does not dispatch a driver or confirm a real fare.
  • Shared posts and feedback depend on Supabase being reachable and its table permissions allowing the request.
  • Marketplace contact opens WhatsApp; the site does not process payments or verify sellers and listings.
  • Campus place labels and route information are a pilot and should be checked with students before being treated as official directions.
  • Community ratings and issue reports are user-submitted and are not independently verified.
